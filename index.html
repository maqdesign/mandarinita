.ART 2026 · PORTFOLIO

An ongoing practice in visual storytelling.

Mandarinita is an independent illustration and comic project rooted in Dominican everyday life.

ARTIST BIOGRAPHY

Mauricio Quezada

Mauricio Quezada is a Dominican illustrator and designer based in Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic. In 2019, he and Yosdina García began Mandarinita, a series of digital comic strips inspired by conversations, office routines, and the small absurdities of daily life. The project’s name grew out of a playful exchange about Yosdina’s favorite fruit, the mandarin orange. Mandarinita was first published on November 16, 2019, and gradually developed a recognizable cast of fruit characters with expressive faces and distinct personalities. Mauricio’s practice connects illustration, character design, graphic communication, and sequential storytelling. Beyond the original comics, the archive documents experiments with animation, stickers, sketches, and related creative formats. The original website, made with Notion, received recognition in the first NotionJam competition in 2022. Mandarinita continues as an independent creative collaboration rooted in Dominican humor and everyday observation.

CREATIVE STATEMENT

Finding the story in an ordinary day.

Mandarinita explores how ordinary moments become shared stories. A complicated Monday, a meeting that could have been an email, or a simple misunderstanding over coffee can feel frustrating while it happens and funny when remembered. The series transforms those recognizable experiences into short illustrated narratives, using rounded fruit characters, exaggerated gestures, and direct visual humor. Dominican expressions and workplace culture give the project its local voice, but its emotions are meant to travel across borders. Each character approaches the same world differently: enthusiasm, skepticism, impatience, or a calmer response can change the meaning of a small event. My creative process begins by noticing a familiar detail, finding the unexpected turn, and reducing the scene to the few expressions and frames needed to communicate it. The goal is not to deny the difficulties of everyday life; it is to offer a little space to recognize ourselves and laugh together.

La historia detrás de la fruta

Un universo nacido de una conversación.

Mandarinita® comenzó como una serie de tiras cómicas digitales creadas por Mauricio Quezada y Yosdina García, publicadas desde el 16 de noviembre de 2019. El proyecto creció alrededor del humor cotidiano dominicano, los personajes frutales y el gusto de contar historias dibujadas.
