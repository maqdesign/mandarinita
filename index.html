El arte detrás de los gajos

Una historia que comenzó dibujando

Mandarinita® comenzó como una serie de tiras cómicas digitales creadas por Mauricio Quezada y Yosdina García, publicadas desde el 16 de noviembre de 2019. Nació de una conversación en la que Yosdina animaba a Mauricio a compartir sus dibujos y mencionaron su fruta favorita: la mandarina.

Desde entonces, el proyecto ha explorado el humor de oficina y la vida cotidiana dominicana mediante personajes frutales expresivos. Su primera página web, creada con Notion, obtuvo el premio del primer NotionJam en 2022.

Artist profile · English

Mauricio Quezada is a Dominican illustrator and designer based in Santo Domingo. Together with Yosdina García, he started Mandarinita in 2019 as a series of digital comic strips inspired by everyday life, office routines, and a shared sense of humor. Their conversations about drawing and Yosdina's favorite fruit gave the project its name. Over time, the idea grew into a universe of expressive fruit characters, playful visual storytelling, animated experiments, and ideas for merchandise. Grounded in Dominican language and culture, Mandarinita presents recognizable emotions and situations through simple shapes and lively expressions. A website built around the project won the first NotionJam competition in 2022. Mauricio continues to develop Mandarinita as an independent creative project, combining visual design, illustration, and sequential storytelling to bring a little lightness to the ordinary moments we all share.

Creative statement · English

Mandarinita turns everyday observations into small visual stories. A meeting that could have been an email, a sleepy Monday morning, or a coffee break can reveal something funny about the way we live and work. Through colorful fruit characters, rounded silhouettes, and exaggerated expressions, the comics aim to make familiar situations instantly recognizable. Their humor grows from the language, pace, and warmth of Dominican daily life, but the underlying feelings are universal: fatigue, impatience, optimism, and the relief of laughing together. Each character brings a different perspective to the same ordinary world, creating a playful space where little frustrations can become shared jokes. The project began as an invitation to draw more often and evolved into an ongoing creative practice. Its ambition is not only to entertain, but to remind readers that a lighter point of view can transform even the most routine day.
